## Overview

The Checkweigher is a highly accurate machine utilizing high-speed digital signals to achieve an accuracy
of **±0.3 g**. Equipped with a full-color touch screen, this user-friendly system supports **eight languages**
and is designed for easy cleaning and maintenance. Its sloped surfaces minimize liquid retention and
discourage bacterial contamination. The Checkweigher can be rapidly dismantled, cleaned, and reassembled,
minimizing downtime.

Conveyor materials in direct food contact comply with **Regulation (EC) 1935/2004**, **Regulation (EU) 10/2011**,
and **FDA** regulations.

**Optional**: waterproof stainless steel frame、scanner system、labeling & spray inkjet coding machine
