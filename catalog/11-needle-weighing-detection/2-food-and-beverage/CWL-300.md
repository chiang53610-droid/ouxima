## Overview

**Streamlined Weighing & Labeling Solution.**

The OSHIMA CWL-300 is a conveyor-type high-speed weigh checker and automatic labeling machine designed to
reduce errors and increase efficiency in factory operations. Ideal for food, farming, and other processing
industries, this flexible system is easy to install, operate, and clean. With a weighing speed detection max
of up to **40 pieces per minute** and the ability to set **50 parameters**, the CWL-300 ensures consistent product
quality while minimizing waste from label reprinting.

The labeling system prints and applies labels immediately after measurement, and can be used as a standalone
machine or adjusted for integration with multiple production lines. It addresses common production problems
such as pre-printed label waste and the lack of metal detection and weighing systems.
