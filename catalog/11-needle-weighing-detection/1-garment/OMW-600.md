## Overview

**Streamlined Safety & Quality Control.**

The needle detection and check weighing system is a vital tool for businesses requiring final inspections
before shipping products. This combined system ensures the detection of hidden metal objects such as
scissors, paper clips, or other metal parts within cartons, while also checking if the shipping package is
too heavy or too light. With a small footprint, the system can be easily integrated into existing
production lines — food and beverage weighing, clothing needle detection, and weighing scales.

- **Standard**: no automated reject device
- **Optional**: automated reject device

**Advantages**: supports sorting / packing、mixed barcode scanning、auto-reversing conveyor with alarm on detection
**Real-time monitoring**: data input & output、camera and save
**User interface**: flat panel touch screen、easy-to-use、multilingual interface
**Investment cost**: easy installation and use、cost-effective maintenance、integrable with other devices

**產線組成**：Non-power conveyor → Power conveyor → Incline belt conveyor → Needle detector → Check weigher → Pushing rejector
