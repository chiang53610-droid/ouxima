## Overview

**Simplify labor work.**

This system allows mixed barcodes and offers moving object reading (light reflection, crease
identification), as well as save features, data export, report production, and configurable conveyor speed.
It may be equipped with other OSHIMA quality control production solutions and is well-suited for the
garment industry's barcodes (1D code, 2D code). It enables continuous management and data traceability,
saving time and money.
