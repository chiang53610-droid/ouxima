## Overview

**Data-driven control for garment safety.**

ON-RFID is designed for data-driven apparel management. It enables precise inventory tracking and
automatic record linking. Each garment's RFID tag synchronizes with detection results, creating a complete
historical database that strengthens traceability and quality control. With ON-RFID, factories can move
beyond manual checks and achieve smarter, more reliable production oversight.

可與檢針機串接使用：**ON-RFID + ON-688CDD6S**（RFID + Digital Conveyor Needle Detector）。
