## Overview

**Offering precise label application and improving production efficiency.**

The OSHIMA labeling machine is a highly efficient solution designed to replace traditional plotter paper by
directly printing essential identification information on the top fabric layer. This system automates label
application during fabric spreading, marking crucial details such as marker width, length, and starting
points for the cutter. It also prints barcodes and QR codes, enhancing traceability and production accuracy.

Maintenance-free and precise in sticker positioning — even for smaller parts — the machine significantly
reduces manual labor and operational costs.
