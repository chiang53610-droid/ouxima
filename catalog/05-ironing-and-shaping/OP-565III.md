## Overview

OP-565III is the advanced model combining both **OP-120T** and **OP-535** — the ideal choice for premium
shirt manufacturers.

An automatic all-in-one collar trim, turn, and shaping machine. Featuring the ability to trim, turn, and
press collars, this machine automatically feeds and collects materials and offers the choice of single or
double pressing stages depending on fabric requirements. With quick mold replacement and intuitive, easy
operation without the need for skilled workers, this machine also has a cooling function to prevent iron
marks in case of high temperatures.

**Output**: 180–200 pieces per hour.
