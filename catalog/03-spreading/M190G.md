## Overview

**Let technology handle the work.**

Having robotic arms in a factory warehouse can boost efficiency and cut down on labor costs. They can
perform tasks like picking up materials and transporting them to the spreader with precision and accuracy,
reducing the need for human intervention. Robotic arms can work around the clock without rest, leading to
increased productivity and throughput. With PLC operation, the robotic gripper can pick up materials from
multi-level shelving units with ease, making the process more streamlined and efficient.

**Advantages**
- Works continuously without rest
- Reduced labor costs
- Comfortable lifting big objects — maximizes productivity
- Safety in dangerous settings — decreased risk of human error
- Picks up flat-folded textiles

**User interface**: touch screen, user-interface-compliant, easy programming
**Investment cost**: simple installation, low maintenance costs, data connectivity
